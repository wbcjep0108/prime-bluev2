# Stacked Cards Image Assets

Put the image assets for your 4 stacked cards here.

Recommended names and dimensions:
- Card 1 (Blue Raspberry): `card1.jpg` (or `card1.png`, `card1.webp`)
- Card 2 (Zero Sugar Formula): `card2.jpg`
- Card 3 (Champions Proof): `card3.jpg`
- Card 4 (Prime Energy): `card4.jpg`

### Dimensions & Optimization
- Aspect Ratio: **16:9** or **4:3** recommended (landscape orientation, e.g., `1200 x 900` pixels).
- The left column of the card takes exactly 40% of the card's width.
- The image will automatically cover the left side (`object-fit: cover`).
